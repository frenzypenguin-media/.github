<div align="center">

**FrenzyPenguin Media is where the music gets filmed.** We are an electronic-music
artist collective and production house: we record and stream DJ sets and live
performances, cut captioned video for them, and build the art videos and visual
identities that make an act recognisable before the first track drops.

</div>

## 🧬 The research branch

Not everything here is a dance floor. One branch of the studio tracks the
breakthroughs that will shape the next decade, and scores them against primary
sources.

<div align="center">

### 🧬 **[transhumanists](https://github.com/transhumanists)** — human progress, quantified

</div>

Seven verticals — biotechnology, computing and AGI, quantum physics, renewable energy,
cybersecurity, spaceflight, and defence. Around 80 feeds are scraped, scored and
pinned on a live world map every six hours. Every claim keeps its source and its date;
if the source cannot be reached, the record leaves the map.

[![Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/live%20dashboard-transhumanists.github.io-7C4DFF?style=for-the-badge&logo=githubpages)](https://transhumanists.github.io)

## 🤝 Take part

There are four ways in, and all four are open.

- 🎤 **Play the stage.** Come to [Open Stage Island](https://openstageisland.github.io), or ask for a slot — the stage is free and always open.
- 🎥 **Join the stream.** Subscribe on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/FrenzyPenguinMedia?sub_confirmation=1) or [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/frenzypenguin.media), and say hello in chat.
- 🎬 **Send a video.** Got a set, a visual piece, a livestream you want captioned or cut? [Open an issue](https://github.com/frenzypenguin-media/frenzypenguin-media.github.io/issues) and tell us what you are making.
- 🧩 **Build with us.** The tooling is open source. Pick an open issue on any repo above, or read [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/frenzypenguin-media/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and start there.

**For everyone else here:** newcomers, LGBTQI+ residents and furries are all welcome —
on the island and in the repos. Read the [code of conduct](https://github.com/frenzypenguin-media/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) first; it is short and it is enforced.

**Found a vulnerability?** Report it **privately** — never as a public issue. Use the
**Security** tab of the repository, or email `security@neohiro.io`. See `SECURITY.md`.
